Low EC Cocopeat (Loose)

Product Overview

Low EC Loose Cocopeat is a specially processed coir pith designed to have a low and controlled concentration of soluble salts. It is produced from coconut husk through processes such as washing, screening and drying, with additional treatment where required.

The low-salinity profile makes it suitable for seed propagation, nurseries, greenhouse cultivation, potting mixes and hydroponic growing media, where excessive salts can interfere with plant nutrient uptake.

Unlike compressed cocopeat blocks, this product is supplied in loose, ready-to-use form.

What Does "Low EC" Mean?

EC (Electrical Conductivity) indicates the concentration of dissolved ions or soluble salts in a material or growing medium.

A lower EC generally means there are fewer soluble salts present.

This is particularly important when cocopeat is used with fertigation or hydroponic nutrient solutions, because excessive salts can contribute to an unnecessarily high root-zone EC.

Target EC: ≤ 0.5 mS/cm

Applications

🌱 Seed Germination

Low EC cocopeat is useful for seedlings because young plants can be more sensitive to excessive soluble salts.

🌿 Nurseries

Suitable as a component of nursery growing media for vegetables, flowers and ornamental plants.

💧 Hydroponics

Low EC cocopeat can be used as a hydroponic growing medium when its EC, pH, particle size and mineral profile meet the requirements of the crop and cultivation system.

Buffered low-EC cocopeat is generally the more appropriate premium product when precise nutrient management is required.

🪴 Potting Mixes

Can be blended with compost, soil, perlite, coco chips or other materials to create customized growing media.

🏡 Home Gardening

Suitable for gardeners who want a clean, low-salt cocopeat component for potting mixtures.
