Washed Cocopeat (Loose)

Product Overview

Washed Loose Cocopeat is a refined coir pith product made from coconut husk after the extraction of coir fibre. The cocopeat undergoes a controlled washing process to remove excess soluble salts and impurities, followed by draining and drying.

It retains the natural porous structure of cocopeat while offering lower and more consistent salinity than unwashed cocopeat. It is supplied in loose form and can be used directly in horticultural applications or as an ingredient in professional growing-media blends.

Key Features

Its fine structure and moisture-retention properties make it useful in seed-starting mixtures.

💧 Hydroponic Growing Media

Washed cocopeat can be used in hydroponic applications when its EC and other quality parameters meet the requirements of the crop and growing system. For sensitive crops and professional hydroponics, buffered cocopeat is generally preferred.
