Product Overview

Premium Buffered Cocopeat Blocks are high-quality, compressed growing media blocks manufactured from carefully processed coconut husk. The cocopeat is washed, buffered, and dried before compression, helping reduce excess soluble salts and improve the balance of exchangeable minerals.

Why Buffered Cocopeat?

Coconut husk naturally contains soluble and exchangeable mineral ions. If excessive potassium and sodium remain in the growing medium, they can affect nutrient management.

Buffering helps create a more predictable root-zone environment, particularly when growers use carefully controlled fertigation or hydroponic nutrient solutions.

Storage & Handling

Keep compressed blocks dry and protected from rain and excessive humidity. Store on pallets or another clean surface and protect from direct contamination.

To use, place the block in a suitable container, gradually add clean water and allow it to fully hydrate. Once expanded, loosen and mix the material before planting.
