Washed Cocopeat Blocks

Product Overview

Washed Cocopeat Blocks are compressed blocks of cocopeat produced from coconut husk and processed to reduce excess soluble salts and water-soluble impurities. After washing, the material is drained, dried, screened and compressed into convenient blocks.

The compressed format reduces transportation and storage volume, while hydration restores the cocopeat to a loose, porous growing medium with high water retention and good aeration.

Why Choose Washed Cocopeat Blocks?

🧂 Reduced Salinity

Washing reduces soluble salts, resulting in a lower-EC product compared with unwashed cocopeat.

💧 Excellent Water Retention

Cocopeat can hold substantial moisture while maintaining air spaces around plant roots.

📦 Efficient Logistics

Compression reduces the volume of dry material, making the product easier and more economical to store and transport.

🌱 Versatile Growing Medium

After hydration, it can be used as a component of growing media for a wide range of horticultural applications.

Applications

🌱 Nurseries

Suitable for preparing growing media for seedlings and nursery plants, subject to crop requirements.

🪴 Potting Mixes

Can be blended with:

🌿 Horticulture

Suitable for flowers, ornamental plants and other horticultural crops.

💧 Hydroponics

Washed cocopeat may be used in hydroponic systems when its EC, pH and physical properties meet the requirements of the crop.

For professional hydroponic applications requiring precise nutrient management, buffered cocopeat is generally preferred.

Storage & Handling

Store the compressed blocks in a dry, clean and covered location, protected from rain and excessive humidity.

To use, place the block in a suitable container and gradually add water. Allow sufficient time for complete hydration, then loosen the expanded cocopeat before incorporating it into the growing medium.
