Coco Coir Fibre

Product Overview

Coco Coir Fibre is a natural, renewable fibre extracted from the fibrous husk of mature coconuts. It is obtained during the mechanical processing of coconut husks and is known for its strength, durability, resilience and resistance to moisture.

Coir fibre is available in different forms and grades depending on fibre length, thickness, colour, cleanliness and processing method. It can be supplied as long fibre, medium fibre, short fibre or baled fibre for different industrial applications.

Types of Coco Coir Fibre

1. Long Coir Fibre

Long, relatively strong fibres with good length and flexibility.

Applications:

Ropes

Mats

Brushes

Geotextiles

Twine

2. Medium Coir Fibre

Intermediate-length fibres used in various fibre products and composites.

3. Short Coir Fibre

Shorter fibres and fibre fragments generated during processing.

Applications:

Composite materials

Filling materials

Horticultural products

Fibreboard and other processed products

4. Brown Coir Fibre

Obtained primarily from mature coconut husks. It is generally stronger and more durable.

5. White Coir Fibre

Obtained from younger coconut husks and is generally finer and more flexible.

Applications

🧶 Ropes & Twine

Coir's strength and flexibility make it suitable for ropes, twine and cordage.

🪢 Mats & Rugs

Used to manufacture door mats, floor mats, runners and other woven products.

🌱 Horticulture

Coir fibre can be incorporated into growing media and used to improve structure and aeration.

🌿 Geotextiles

Coir fibre is widely used to manufacture biodegradable erosion-control products such as:

Coir nets

Coir blankets

Erosion-control mats

Geotextile rolls

These products can help stabilize soil and support vegetation establishment.

🏭 Composite Materials

Coir fibre can be used as a natural reinforcement fibre in certain composite materials.

🧹 Brushes

Its natural stiffness makes it suitable for various brushes and cleaning products.

🪑 Furniture & Upholstery

Processed coir fibre can be used as a natural filling material in some mattresses, cushions, and upholstery products.

Storage

Store coir fibre in a clean, dry and well-ventilated location. Protect it from rain, excessive humidity, soil contamination and sources of ignition.
