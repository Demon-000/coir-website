Unwashed Cocopeat Blocks

Product Overview

Unwashed Cocopeat Blocks are compressed blocks made from natural cocopeat (coir pith) obtained during coconut-husk processing. The cocopeat is screened, dried, and compressed without undergoing washing or buffering.

Compression reduces the volume of the product for efficient storage, handling and transportation. When water is added, the block expands into loose cocopeat suitable for a variety of horticultural and agricultural applications.

Key Features

Made from natural coconut husk

Renewable and biodegradable

Compressed for efficient transportation

High natural water-holding capacity

Naturally porous structure

Lightweight

Suitable for bulk and industrial applications

Can be further washed and buffered if required

Cost-effective compared with treated cocopeat

Production Process

Coconut Husk↓Coir Fibre Extraction↓Cocopeat Separation↓Screening↓Drying↓Quality Inspection↓Compression↓

Unwashed Cocopeat Block

Unlike washed or buffered products, no dedicated salt-removal or calcium-buffering treatment is carried out.

Applications

🪴 Potting Mixes

Can be used as a component of potting mixtures after appropriate preparation and testing.

🌳 Landscaping

Suitable for general landscaping and soil-conditioning applications where the required EC specifications permit its use.

🌱 Horticulture

Can be incorporated into growing media for suitable plants.

🏭 Further Processing

Ideal as a raw material for manufacturers producing:

Washed cocopeat

Buffered cocopeat

Low-EC cocopeat

Grow bags

Cocopeat blends

♻️ Composting

Can be incorporated into composting and organic-material processing systems.

Storage

Store in a dry, covered and clean environment, preferably on pallets. Protect the blocks from rain and excessive humidity before use.
