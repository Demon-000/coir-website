Premium Low EC Cocopeat Blocks

Product Overview

Low EC Cocopeat Blocks are specially processed, compressed cocopeat products designed to provide a low and controlled level of soluble salts. The cocopeat is washed and processed to reduce excess salts, then dried, screened, and compressed into blocks.

When hydrated, the block expands into a lightweight, porous growing medium with excellent water retention, aeration and root-zone structure.

Low EC cocopeat is particularly useful where growers need greater control over the nutrient concentration of the growing medium, including nurseries, seed propagation, greenhouse cultivation and hydroponic systems.

What Does Low EC Mean?

Electrical Conductivity (EC) is an indicator of the concentration of dissolved ions or soluble salts in a growing medium.

For cocopeat, a lower EC means lower soluble salt content, which can be beneficial when growers are carefully controlling nutrients through irrigation or fertigation.

EC ≤ 0.5 mS/cm

Storage & Hydration

Store blocks in a dry, covered and clean environment, away from rain and excessive moisture.

For use:

Place block → Add clean water → Allow full hydration → Break apart → Mix → Use

The amount of water required and final expansion volume depend on the block's compression and moisture characteristics.
