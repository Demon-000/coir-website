Cocopeat Grow Bags

Product Overview

Cocopeat Grow Bags are ready-to-use growing media made from processed coconut coir pith and packed in durable, UV-resistant polyethylene bags. They provide a lightweight, porous root environment with a combination of water retention, aeration, and drainage, making them suitable for greenhouse, nursery, and soilless cultivation.

Grow bags can be supplied using washed, low-EC or buffered cocopeat, depending on the crop and customer's growing requirements.

Available Grades

🟤 Washed Cocopeat Grow Bags

Designed for general horticultural and greenhouse applications with reduced soluble salts compared with unwashed cocopeat.

🟢 Low EC Cocopeat Grow Bags

Manufactured for applications where low soluble salt levels are important, such as seedlings and controlled growing systems.

🔵 Buffered Cocopeat Grow Bags

Washed and calcium-buffered for applications requiring a more controlled root-zone environment, particularly professional greenhouse and hydroponic cultivation.
