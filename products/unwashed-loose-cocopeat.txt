Unwashed Loose Cocopeat

Product Overview

Unwashed Loose Cocopeat is a natural coir pith product obtained as a by-product during the mechanical processing of coconut husks for coir fibre extraction. It consists primarily of fine particles of coconut husk and has a naturally porous structure.

The material is screened and processed without undergoing washing or chemical buffering, preserving its natural mineral content. It is supplied in loose form and can be used directly for general horticultural applications or as a raw material for further processing.

Typical Applications

1. Potting MixesCan be blended with compost, soil, perlite, coco chips or other growing-media components.

2. Soil ConditioningCan help improve the physical structure and moisture-retention characteristics of soil when appropriately incorporated.

3. NurseriesUsed in the preparation of growing media for plants and seedlings, subject to crop requirements and EC testing.

4. LandscapingSuitable for general landscaping and horticultural applications where low-salt growing media is not specifically required.

5. CompostingIts organic nature makes it suitable as a carbon-rich component in composting and organic-material processing.

6. Further ProcessingCan be washed, screened, dried, and buffered to manufacture higher-specification cocopeat.
