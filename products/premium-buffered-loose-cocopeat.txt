Premium Buffered Loose Cocopeat

Product Overview

Buffered Loose Cocopeat is a premium coir pith growing medium produced from coconut husk. It undergoes washing followed by a controlled buffering treatment, typically using a calcium-based solution, to reduce excess soluble salts and improve the balance of exchangeable minerals in the cocopeat.

The result is a clean, low-salinity and more stable growing medium suitable for professional horticulture, nurseries, greenhouse cultivation and hydroponic applications.

Unlike compressed cocopeat blocks, loose buffered cocopeat is supplied in its expanded, ready-to-use form, making it convenient for customers who do not want to hydrate and expand compressed blocks.

During buffering, calcium ions are introduced to the cocopeat's cation-exchange sites. This helps displace excessive potassium and sodium, which can then be removed during subsequent rinsing.

Why Buffering Is Important

Coconut husk naturally contains exchangeable and soluble mineral ions, particularly potassium (K⁺) and sodium (Na⁺).

If these are present at excessive levels, they can interfere with nutrient management in sensitive crops.

Buffering helps to:

Reduce excess K⁺ and Na⁺ → Increase Ca²⁺ occupancy of exchange sites → Improve nutrient stability → Produce a more predictable growing medium

This is particularly valuable in hydroponic and greenhouse cultivation, where growers precisely control the nutrient solution.
